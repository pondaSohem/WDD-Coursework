What is the Goal>

Equal access to affordable education ensures that everyone, regardless of their background, has the opportunity to gain skills and knowledge through technical training, vocational programs, and higher education.

Why it matters

  • Reduces Unemployment
  • Promotes Equality
  • Builds skilled workforce

Types of Education

Educations is not just about books and copies they are of types based on how performative they are.
Such as:

  • Technical Education (engineering, IT, etc.)
  • Vocational Education (practical skills like carpentry, plumbing)
  • Higher Education (universities, degrees)

Barriers

Many face challenges and can not over come it.The challenges one faces:

  • High tuition fees
  • Lack of institutions in rural areas
  • Gender inequality
  • Limited scholarships
  • Digital divide

The way Forward

With countless problems and challaenges in the way but united and together support for equal education and careful investment in the idea of education for all and ensure that no one ever has to question,"Why can one not be educated."
